# Research and sources

[Русский](SOURCES.md) | English

Reviewed on October 29, 2026. These writing articles and documentation explain decisions in the skill. Instructions and examples were written for this project. Its license does not cover external sources.

## Writing, voice and material

- Maxim Ilyakhov, [Who, what and why?](https://maximilyahov.ru/blog/all/who-why/) (Russian): begin with the reader and the action useful to them. This informs a short brief rather than mandatory embellishments.
- Maxim Ilyakhov, [How to write without material?](https://maximilyahov.ru/blog/all/ask-zero-resource/) (Russian): polished editing cannot replace missing substance. Search or clarify when necessary facts are absent.
- Maxim Ilyakhov, [A writing workflow](https://maximilyahov.ru/blog/all/algoritm-raboty-nad-tekstom/) (Russian): research, context and intent precede polishing. The skill condenses these decisions into a practical pass.
- [Mailchimp: Voice and Tone](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/): a recognizable voice can persist while tone changes with the situation. This informs personal, expert and brand writing.
- Kate Moran, [The Four Dimensions of Tone of Voice](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tone-of-voice-dimensions/), NN/g: formality, humor, respect and enthusiasm help choose a tone; they are not four mandatory questionnaire fields.
- [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing): editors' observations about recurring language and structure. Used as candidates for editorial review. A stylistic feature alone does not establish authorship.

Clear-writing principles do not become sentence-length limits, joke quotas or mandatory closing calls to action. Adaptation follows the task, audience and intended use. A platform name does not determine one deliverable.

## Headlines, opening lines and participation

Reread for [issue #1](https://github.com/WhyFaust/chelovechno/issues/1) on October 30, 2026:

- [NN/G, Microcontent (2017)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/microcontent-how-to-write-headlines-page-titles-and-subject-lines/): titles that stand alone, convey specific information and put meaningful words early.
- [Meta, Fighting Engagement Bait on Facebook (2017, with updates)](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/): manufactured reactions differ from substantive requests for help or advice.

Applying these ideas to opening lines in authored posts is the project's editorial choice. Meta's article is historical and about Facebook, not current Threads or X ranking. Neither source establishes increased reach or a universal opening formula.

## Post structure and reader action

Read on October 30, 2026, from the author's suggested material:

- Viktoria Pinina and the SMMplanner editors, [How to write social posts: examples](https://smmplanner.com/blog/kak-pisat-tekst-dlya-postov-v-socialnyh-setyah/) (May 6, 2020, Russian): a main point, a logical sequence of reader questions and removal of empty phrasing.
- Dmitry Zverev, [Two structures for social posts](https://zverinfo.ru/2-struktury-postov/) (June 5, 2026, Russian): useful content leading to an offer and a poll with a clear response method. These inform optional structures in the reference.
- Maryana Ishkhnelidze, ZHIR, [How to write a social post](https://zhir.media/kak-napisat-post-v-soczseti-instrukcziya/) (Russian): connecting the author's purpose, reader interest and supporting material; meaningful paragraphs and lists.

Project decisions: the useful section should deliver without a purchase, and avoiding reaction bait should not remove a functional call to action. The skill does not adopt the articles' 70/30 ratio, item and sentence quotas, mandatory calls to action, reach advice or platform-limit tables. New examples use our own fictional briefs rather than copying source headlines or posts as templates.

## Format and freshness

Version 0.2.0 removed the platform-by-platform handbook. Exact limits and markup are checked against current official documentation for the relevant field and use when they affect the result. Platform names in examples provide brief context.

Freshness rules are project decisions: a default 72-hour news window, primary sources, separate event and publication dates, rollout qualifications and source notes. Article, speech and historical research use the depth and period their task requires. This is a verification procedure, not a guarantee that every found source is true. Search filters and page update dates alone do not establish freshness.

Documentation and platforms change. Reopen the relevant primary source before changing a claim about limits or installation.
